We offer access to a single platform covering Mutual Funds, PMS, AIFs, SIFs, Bonds & NCDs, SIPs, Fixed Income Solutions, Loans, and GIFT City opportunities. Your mix is selected based on your financial goals, time horizon, and risk appetite � not a one-size-fits-all product.

A Mutual Fund pools money from many investors into a professionally managed scheme (regulated by SEBI), and you can start with amounts as low as a few hundred rupees. A Portfolio Management Service (PMS) is a personalized portfolio � usually for a minimum of ?50 lakh � where a dedicated portfolio manager invests directly in selected stocks and bonds based on your mandate and risk profile. Both are SEBI-regulated, but PMS offers customization in exchange for higher minimums.

Yes. Through a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) you can begin with as little as ?500 per month. It is automated, uses rupee-cost averaging to smooth market ups and downs, and you can increase (step-up) the amount over time as your income grows � small, consistent contributions build a meaningful corpus through compounding.

GIFT City in Gandhinagar, Gujarat is India's first International Financial Services Centre (IFSC). It enables foreign-currency savings and investments, access to global funds and capital markets, and several tax incentives designed for international finance. Eligible investors can diversify beyond domestic markets here � we help you evaluate whether GIFT City opportunities fit your portfolio.

For equity-oriented funds, short-term capital gains (holding up to 1 year) are taxed at 20%, while long-term gains above ?1.25 lakh are taxed at 12.5% as per Budget 2024 rules. Debt funds are taxed as per your income-tax slab. Holding periods, TDS, and frequent rule changes matter � always verify current tax provisions with a chartered accountant before filing.

A Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) lets you redeem a fixed amount from your fund at regular intervals � acting like a monthly pension. It suits retirees or anyone wanting regular income while keeping capital invested. An SWP can be paired with an ongoing SIP, and the withdrawals are taxed only on the redeemed growth portion.

KYC requires your PAN, Aadhaar, bank account details, and a valid mobile number/email. The process itself is paperless: an in-person verification or Aadhaar eKYC with OTP, a FATCA/CRS declaration, and a risk-profile questionnaire. For SIPs, you also sign a one-time NACH auto-debit mandate. We assist you through the entire onboarding.

No investment is completely risk-free. Bonds and NCDs carry credit risk (the issuer's ability to pay) and interest-rate risk, while bank FDs are insured up to ?5 lakh per bank under DICGC. Higher-rated paper lowers default risk but also returns. We match the tenure and credit quality of fixed-income instruments to your goals so the risk stays within your comfort level.
